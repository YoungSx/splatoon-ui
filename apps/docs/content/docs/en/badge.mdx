## Usage

`Badge` delivers authentic in-game visual archetypes from Splatoon 3:

- **Torn-paper Badge (default)**: Classic jagged torn-edge paper look supporting 6 vibrant ink color tokens (yellow, blue, green, red, purple, monochrome) for short labels and category markers.
- **Pin / Splashtag Achievement Badges**: High-fidelity reproduction of in-game Splashtag metal relief pins (1:1 aspect ratio with transparent backgrounds and authentic gold, silver, and bronze metallic rims & specular gloss). Built-in official presets (`judd`, `cohozuna`, `steelhead`, `big-run`, `splattershot`) and tier ranks (`gold`, `silver`, `bronze`), with full support for custom badge images via `src`.
- **Dot / Exclamation Alert Bubble**: Faithful recreation of in-game Lobby Terminal, Jukebox, and Smallfry alert bubbles featuring saturated ink exclamation marks (`!`), supporting automated corner placement when wrapping trigger elements.

## Design Notes

- **Concise Copy**: Keep text labels to one or two words to preserve the punchy Splatoon street aesthetic.
- **Splashtag Pins**: Achievement pins use 1:1 square dimensions with transparent backgrounds, metallic bevels, and glare highlights matching official Nintendo game assets.
- **Corner Anchoring**: When using `dot` prop around child elements, the badge anchors automatically to the top-right corner by default (`placement="top-right"`).
