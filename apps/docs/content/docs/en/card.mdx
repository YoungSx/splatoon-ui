## Usage

Use Card when content needs a clear surface, title, description, and action area. The subcomponents keep spacing consistent across variants.

## Design Notes

- Use `paper` for neutral content.
- Use `staple` and `torn` for stronger editorial surfaces.
- Avoid nesting full cards inside other cards.
