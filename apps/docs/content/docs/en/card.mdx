## Usage

Use Card for neutral content that needs a clear surface, title, description, and action area. Use the dedicated StapleCard and TornCard entrypoints when the visual treatment has its own media, tape, or torn-paper contract.

## Design Notes

- Keep Card content structural: header, body, footer, and actions.
- Use `splatoon-ui/staple-card` for stapled feed cards.
- Use `splatoon-ui/torn-card` for torn-paper editorial surfaces.
- Avoid nesting full cards inside other cards.
