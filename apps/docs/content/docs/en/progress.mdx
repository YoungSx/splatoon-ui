## Overview

The Progress System provides authentic in-game visual components inspired by _Splatoon 3_, including standard single progress meter, Turf War dual-faction confrontation bar, and Tricolor Splatfest meter.

## Components

### 1. Progress (Classic Meter)

Standard single-fill progress meter featuring the signature -12° skew angle, dynamic ink splatter head, and full accessible `role="progressbar"` compliance.

### 2. TurfWarProgress (Dual-Team Confrontation Bar)

Modeled after the official _Splatoon 3_ Judd weigh-in screen and battle replay graph:

- **Dual-Faction Contest**: Left side represents Team Alpha, right side represents Team Bravo.
- **-12° Angle & 3D Center Divider**: Classic skew with solid black 3D central seam separating both inks.
- **Dynamic Team Badges & Percentages**: Displays team badges, formatted values, and central `VS` badge.
- **DANGER! Alert**: Automatically pulses when either side drops below ≤ 35%.

### 3. TricolorProgress (3-Way Splatfest Bar)

Modeled after the official _Splatoon 3_ Tricolor Turf War replay graph:

- **3-Way Proportional Fill**: Attacker 1, Defender, and Attacker 2 segments.
- **Angled 3D Dividers**: Clean -12° black dividers between each team.

## Design Notes

- **In-Game Palettes**: Native support for game color pairs (Yellow/Purple, Cyan/Orange, etc.).
- **Reduced Motion**: Full support for `prefers-reduced-motion` with graceful static fallbacks.
